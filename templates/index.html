eot_threshold
0.5-0.9. Confidence needed to end a turn. Higher is more certain, slower.
eager_eot_threshold
0.3-0.9, unset by default. Setting it enables EagerEndOfTurn and TurnResumed. Must be ≤ eot_threshold.
eot_timeout_ms
500-60000. Silence that forces a turn to end regardless of confidence.
language_hint
flux-general-multi only. On flux-general-en it is rejected at the handshake.
